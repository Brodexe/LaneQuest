# Map slop: roads and urban layout

Applies to: roads, streets, sidewalks, curbs, road markings, plazas, districts, neighborhoods, zoning, access routes.

- **M6** Roads that lead nowhere or intersect nonsensically.
- **M7** Sidewalks, curbs, and road markings that don't line up.
- **M14** Excessively wide streets and empty plazas with nothing to do.
- **M27** Uncanny symmetry: Entire neighborhoods are mirrored or laid out with perfect symmetry where organic development would be expected.
- **M44** Unbelievable urban planning: Residential houses back directly onto loading docks, highways cut through plazas, and industrial facilities lack access roads.
