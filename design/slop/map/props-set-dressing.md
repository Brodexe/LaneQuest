# Map slop: props and set dressing

Applies to: streetlamps, streetlights, benches, trash cans, street furniture, clutter, debris, pipes, cables, power lines, ducts, machinery, abandoned objects, any decorative prop, and any group of similar items (books on shelves, candles, bottles, jars, stacks).

- **M4** Randomly scattered trees, rocks, and props used to fill empty space.
- **M15** Clutter piles made from random junk assets without environmental logic.
- **M16** Identical streetlights, benches, and trash cans repeated at mathematically regular intervals.
- **M39** Prop soup: Every available asset category is crammed into one area, regardless of whether it belongs there.
- **M42** Orphaned infrastructure: Power lines end in midair, pipes terminate without connections, and ventilation ducts lead nowhere.
- **M43** Decorative machinery: Industrial equipment has belts, pipes, and control panels but no coherent physical arrangement or apparent function.
- **M49** Fake environmental storytelling: Random abandoned cars, bloodstains, scattered documents, and overturned furniture suggest events that the surrounding environment doesn't support.
- **M51** Clone rows: Many similar items placed together (books on a shelf, candles on stands, bottles, jars, stacked boxes) are basically the same item repeated. Every such group must be strongly heterogeneous: vary size, proportions, color, material, wear, angle (leaning, toppled, pulled out) and spacing, and include gaps and odd ones out. A row or stack of near-identical copies is never acceptable, even in the background.
