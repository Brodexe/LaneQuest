# Map slop: navigation and composition

Applies to: level section layout, routes, landmarks, sightlines, backdrops, level boundaries, secret areas, in-world labels and arrows, visual hierarchy.

- **M22** Random floating labels, arrows, and icons that break environmental immersion.
- **M23** Generic landmarks (towers, castles, giant statues) added solely to make the map look impressive.
- **M24** Secret rooms, parkour towers, and collectible areas inserted without any connection to the map's identity.
- **M25** A map that looks impressive in a screenshot but has no coherent navigation, spatial logic, or interesting places to explore.
- **M37** Unnatural sightlines: Streets terminate in blank walls, important landmarks are obscured, or long corridors reveal nothing interesting.
- **M48** Boundary concealment: Roads, terrain, or buildings abruptly end at the edge of the playable area, revealing unfinished geometry.
- **M50** No spatial hierarchy: Every building, prop, landmark, and street receives equal visual emphasis, leaving players unable to distinguish important places from background scenery.
