# Map slop: lighting and effects

Applies to: lights, lamps, shadows, fog, bloom, particles, dust, smoke, sparks, glow, atmosphere.

- **M20** Fog, bloom, and particle effects used to conceal unfinished map sections.
- **M46** Lighting contradiction: Strong shadows point in conflicting directions, windows glow during bright daylight, or indoor lighting illuminates spaces through solid walls.
- **M47** Unnatural ambient effects: Dust, smoke, fog, sparks, and floating particles appear uniformly throughout the map without environmental causes.
