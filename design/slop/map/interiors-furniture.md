# Map slop: interiors and furniture

Applies to: room interiors, labs, offices, furniture, desks, benches, beds, tables, chairs.

- **M34** Unusable furniture: Chairs face walls, desks block doors, beds intersect walls, and tables sit at incompatible heights.
- **M45** Contextless interiors: Every building contains the same generic desk, computer, couch, and potted plant regardless of its purpose.
