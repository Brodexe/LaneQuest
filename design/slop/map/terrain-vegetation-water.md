# Map slop: terrain, vegetation, and water

Applies to: terrain, ground, height changes, trees, grass, bushes, water, shorelines.

- **M17** Trees and vegetation placed without regard for terrain, sunlight, or urban layout.
- **M18** Terrain that abruptly changes height or intersects buildings.
- **M19** Water that clips through land, structures, or walkable surfaces.
- **M36** Arbitrary elevation changes: Small height differences force awkward jumps or create invisible barriers across otherwise flat terrain.
