# Map slop: art style, color, and materials

Applies to: colors, materials, textures, decals, neon, glow, art direction, asset choice.

- **M1** Overly vibrant, low-poly graphics with brightly colored enemies.
- **M2** Assets and textures that clash with the rest of the game's visual style.
- **M21** Neon signs and glowing outlines applied indiscriminately to ordinary objects.
- **M29** Texture stretching: Brick, wood, and concrete textures stretch across large surfaces or repeat at visibly unnatural scales.
- **M40** Unmotivated color blocking: Large sections of the map change color abruptly without a change in material, lighting, or environment.
- **M41** Decal overload: Graffiti, posters, stains, warning signs, and stickers are scattered everywhere to simulate detail.
