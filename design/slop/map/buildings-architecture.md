# Map slop: buildings and architecture

Applies to: buildings, facades, walls, windows, doors, stairs, floors, balconies, damage, building placement.

- **M3** Buildings that are just hollow boxes with doors pasted onto the front.
- **M5** Repeated buildings with identical layouts and different colors.
- **M9** Windows that are painted onto walls instead of being actual windows.
- **M10** Doors that lead into solid walls or empty, unfinished interiors.
- **M11** Buildings with no discernible purpose or believable interior layout.
- **M26** Fake depth: Buildings have detailed facades but no actual architectural depth or protruding features.
- **M28** Inconsistent architectural eras: Medieval towers, futuristic glass skyscrapers, suburban homes, and industrial warehouses coexist without any worldbuilding justification.
- **M30** Floating foundations: Buildings sit slightly above the terrain, exposing gaps beneath their foundations.
- **M31** Unconvincing damage: Cracks, rubble, and broken walls appear as decorative decals without corresponding structural damage.
- **M32** Impossible architecture: Staircases lead into ceilings, balconies have no entrances, and rooms have physically implausible dimensions.
- **M33** Window nonsense: Windows are randomly sized, unevenly spaced, or placed where interior floors and ceilings would intersect them.
- **M35** Doorway theater: Doors are placed for visual effect but are too small, blocked, or inaccessible to the player.
