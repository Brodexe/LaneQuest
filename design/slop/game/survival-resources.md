# Game slop: survival meters and resources

Applies to: health, stamina, timers, meters, durability, inventory, carrying (including crates).

- **G14** Stamina bars attached to every physical action.
- **G15** Hunger, thirst, and temperature meters that demand constant maintenance.
- **G16** Survival meters that decay regardless of player activity.
- **G17** Weapon durability that forces repetitive repairs.
- **G18** Inventory weight limits designed to inconvenience players.
