# Game slop: combat and enemies

Applies to: hazards, enemies, bosses, damage, weapons, companions, combat moves, cinematics.

- **G10** Enemies that are just damage sponges.
- **G11** Waves of identical enemies with increasing health.
- **G12** Bosses with oversized health bars and recycled attacks.
- **G13** Generic dodge-roll invincibility frames.
- **G23** Companions that function as walking damage bonuses.
- **G36** Cover-shooting systems with identical encounter layouts.
- **G38** Elemental damage types that amount to color-coded weaknesses.
- **G39** Random critical hits that dominate combat balance.
- **G40** Parry mechanics with excessively generous timing windows.
- **G41** Finishers that interrupt combat for repetitive animations.
- **G42** Quick-time events during cinematic sequences.
