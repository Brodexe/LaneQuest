# Game slop: quests, narrative, and collectibles

Applies to: collectibles, secrets, score bubbles, quests, dialogue, NPCs, story choices, factions, map reveals.

- **G5** Endless fetch quests.
- **G20** Dialogue wheels with superficial choices.
- **G21** Morality systems reduced to good-versus-evil points.
- **G22** Branching narratives that reconverge almost immediately.
- **G24** Faction reputation meters with generic rewards.
- **G25** Map towers that reveal collectible icons.
- **G26** Collectible hunts with no narrative or mechanical significance.
- **G43** Dialogue and interactions gated behind arbitrary character levels.
