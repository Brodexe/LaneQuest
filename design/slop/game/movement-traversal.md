# Game slop: movement and traversal

Applies to: jumping, lane shifting, swinging, ledge grabs, grappling, dashes, wall-running, gliding, mounts, fast travel, any new player ability.

- **G28** Fast travel that replaces interesting traversal.
- **G29** Grappling hooks added solely because they're fashionable.
- **G30** Double jumps and dashes without level-design integration.
- **G31** Generic wall-running.
- **G32** Gliding mechanics that trivialize terrain.
- **G33** Mounts that are just faster movement modes.
